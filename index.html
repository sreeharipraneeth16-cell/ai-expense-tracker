About / Privacy

Where your data lives and exactly when it leaves this browser.

Where your data lives

Transactions, learned category rules, settings and your API key are stored in this browser only. There is no account and no server that keeps your ledger.

When data leaves your browser

  • Categorize with AI: pasted text is masked on your device first. Emails, phone numbers and UPI IDs are replaced; account/card numbers keep only the last 4 digits. The masked text is then sent to Anthropic at api.anthropic.com using claude-haiku-5-5.
  • Scan a bill: if a key is saved, the resized photo is sent to Anthropic as-is. Text inside a photo cannot be masked. On-device scans never leave your browser.
  • Dictation: uses your browser's speech service. Chrome may send audio to Google. The app never records or stores audio.
  • On-device OCR: downloads Tesseract.js from cdn.jsdelivr.net the first time; the photo stays in your browser.
  • No analytics, ads or tracking.

What is never sent

Your saved transactions, category history, backups and lock PIN are never sent anywhere. The API key is sent only in the request header to Anthropic, never inside the message text.

iPhone and SMS

iOS does not let websites or apps read SMS automatically. Copy the bank message from Messages and paste it into AI import. If you run the local relay, an iOS Shortcut can POST text to http://YOUR-PC-IP:8000/api/sms while your phone is on the same Wi-Fi — but iOS still cannot read the SMS for you. The Shortcut needs the message text copied, shared or dictated first.

Live auto-import (optional)

A helper Python script on your computer receives SMS text from a forwarding app on your phone. This page polls that helper every 8 seconds, masks the text, and — only if you have a key — sends it to Anthropic. Messages are stored unencrypted in %TEMP%\expense-tracker-inbox.json on your PC (newest 500), and this page stores only the last-read id. No third-party server is involved beyond Anthropic. The forwarding app may use its own cloud service — check its privacy policy. The helper has no password, so use it only on trusted Wi-Fi and stop it when you are done.

App lock

The optional PIN lock encrypts stored transactions with AES-GCM, using a key derived from your PIN with PBKDF2. If you forget the PIN, the data cannot be recovered unless you have a JSON backup. Backups are plain JSON, so store them safely. App lock protects casual access; it cannot stop someone who fully controls your browser or device.

Backups and clearing data

Use Backup regularly. Clearing site data or using a private window deletes this browser's copy. Delete all my data removes it immediately after confirmation.

How live auto-import works

Off by default. You must turn it on each time.

What it does: a small Python helper on your computer receives SMS text from a forwarding app on your phone. This page asks that helper for new messages every 8 seconds, masks them, and — only if you have an API key — sends them to Anthropic to categorize.

Where messages travel: bank SMS → forwarding app on your phone → expense-server.py on your own PC → this page → Anthropic (only when categorizing).

What is stored: the helper keeps the newest 500 messages, unencrypted, in %TEMP%\expense-tracker-inbox.json. This browser stores only the last-read id. The app never stores the raw SMS in your transaction list.

Third parties: none beyond Anthropic. The helper is your own PC. Your forwarding app may use its own cloud service — check its privacy policy.

The helper has no password and listens on your Wi-Fi network. Anyone on the same network who can reach port 8000 could read or post messages while it runs. Use trusted Wi-Fi only, and stop the helper when you are done.

App lock

Optional PIN. Stored transactions are encrypted with AES-GCM.

Choose a PIN of 4–12 digits. The app will ask for it each time it opens. Encryption happens in your browser; the PIN is never stored.

If you forget this PIN, the data cannot be recovered unless you have a JSON backup. Backups are not encrypted.

App lock is on. Your transactions are encrypted at rest and the app asks for your PIN when it opens.

Turn off lock? The data stays, but it will no longer be encrypted.
